# Privacy Policy — Feel The Beat

_Last updated: October 29, 2026_

Feel The Beat ("the app") is built to work entirely on your device. This policy covers the website and the iPhone and Android apps.

## The short version
- **We don't collect any personal data.**
- **Nothing you hear is recorded, stored, or sent anywhere.**
- No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.

## Microphone
In **Listen** mode, the app asks for microphone access so it can detect the beat of music around you. In the iPhone and Android apps, this is the system's microphone permission. The audio is analyzed live, in memory, on your device, and thrown away right after. It is never recorded, saved, or sent to us or anyone else. You can turn off microphone access at any time in your phone or browser settings. The app's other modes don't use the microphone.

## Songs you choose
In **Song** mode, the file you pick is played and analyzed on your device only. It is never uploaded.

## Settings and storage
The app saves your settings (such as buzz strength and tempo) on your device so they're there next time, and may keep its own files so it works offline. It doesn't store anything that identifies you, and none of it is sent anywhere.

## Vibration
The app uses your phone's vibration motor. This needs no personal data.

## Hosting
The web version is hosted on Netlify (and a copy on GitHub Pages). Like any website host, they may log basic technical information (such as IP addresses) when the page loads. See [Netlify's Privacy Policy](https://www.netlify.com/privacy/) and [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). The iPhone and Android apps don't load anything from the internet.

## Children
The app doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
