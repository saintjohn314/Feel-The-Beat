# Feel The Beat: music for the deaf

Feel The Beat turns music into vibration and light so deaf and hard-of-hearing people can feel every beat.

**Open the app:** https://saintjohn314.github.io/Feel-The-Beat/

## Modes
- **Listen** – uses your phone's microphone to feel the beat of music playing around you (concerts, cars, parties).
- **Song** – pick a song from your phone, or tap **Demo beat**, and feel it as it plays.
- **Metronome** – a steady beat from 40–220 BPM, with a stronger buzz on beat 1.

## Settings
- **Sensitivity** – how easily a sound counts as a beat.
- **Buzz** – Light, Medium or Strong vibration.
- **Flash** – Off, Orb (gentle pulse), or Screen (full-screen flash, behind a photosensitivity warning and limited to 3 flashes per second).

## Phone support
- **Android (Chrome):** full vibration on every beat.
- **iPhone (Safari):** iOS does not let websites vibrate. On iOS 18+ the app uses a light system haptic tap, plus the visual pulse. A full-strength buzz on iPhone needs a native App Store build.

Tip: open the link and choose **Add to Home Screen** to use it like an app.

## Photosensitivity warning
Screen Flash mode flashes the whole screen. It can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy. It is off by default.
