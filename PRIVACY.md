# Privacy Policy — Feel The Beat

_Last updated: September 24, 2026_

Feel The Beat ("the app") is built to work entirely on your device. This policy explains what that means.

## The short version
- **We don't collect any personal data.**
- **Nothing you hear is recorded, stored, or sent anywhere.**
- No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.

## Microphone
In **Listen** mode, the app asks for microphone access so it can detect the beat of music around you. The audio is analyzed live, in memory, on your device, and thrown away right after. It is never recorded, saved, or sent to us or anyone else. You can turn off microphone access at any time in your phone or browser settings. The app's other modes don't use the microphone.

## Songs you choose
In **Song** mode, the file you pick is played and analyzed on your device only. It is never uploaded.

## Settings and storage
The app may keep its own files on your device (for example, so it works offline). It doesn't store anything that identifies you.

## Hosting
The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any website host, GitHub may log basic technical information (such as IP addresses) when the page loads. See [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Children
The app doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
