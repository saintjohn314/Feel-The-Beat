Photosensitivity warning

Screen Flash makes the whole screen flash white on every beat. Flashing lights can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy. If you or anyone watching has epilepsy or feels discomfort, dizziness or blurred vision, stop right away.

Feel music through your phone

Feel The Beat listens for the beat and turns it into a buzz you feel and a pulse you see.

Tips

  • Hold the phone, or keep it in a pocket.
  • Listen: put the phone near the speaker. Strong bass works best.
  • Feel › Bass: the buzz follows the bass line instead of each beat.
  • Metronome: tap the circle to set the tempo.
  • No buzz? Turn on vibration in your phone's settings, and turn off Battery Saver or Low Power Mode.

Private: everything happens on your phone. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.
